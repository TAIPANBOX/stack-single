# stack-single 1.12.0

A minor release that moves the record seal to trailryx-node v1.6.0. The record plane now writes a closing record for a hold wardryx closed by its lifetime (`approval_expired`, read as a timeout) or that its own agent withdrew (`approval_withdrawn`, read as no verdict); under v1.5.0 both were refused as unknown types. Neither reads as a denial. The image also carries the lockfile that clears every advisory with a reachable fix.

## Upgrade first

1. **Run the installer.** No new file, no new setting, no new network. One image moves by tag and digest. The rollback index stays 1.
2. **Going back to 1.11.0 needs nothing special.** The rollback index stays 1. Records written under v1.6.0 carry mapper version 115 and stay valid; v1.5.0 refuses the two types again from the next line it reads.

## Images

| Image | 1.11.0 | 1.12.0 |
|---|---|---|
| trailryx-node (the record seal) | v1.5.0 `f15215cd` | v1.6.0 `68c413c2` |

Every other image is the one 1.11.0 pins. The digest was read from the registry with `docker buildx imagetools inspect` and its provenance verified with `gh attestation verify`; the installer verifies each signature against its release workflow before it pulls.

## What is new

- **The record seal pin**: trailryx-node v1.6.0 in `compose.yaml` and `components.json`. The seal's Keyryx signer, its pinned root kid and its credential are unchanged. The gates that name the pin (the `images-are-digest-pinned` cases of `gates-have-teeth.sh`) read the new one.

## Evidence

- CI on the release head: every install leg, `browser`, `upgrade` and `upgrade-tool-hold`, on a real Docker.
