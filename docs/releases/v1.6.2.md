# stack-single 1.6.2

A patch release. The box moves to typryx v0.6.0, whose `openai-logprobs` backend reaches OpenAI's current models, and the three settings those models need now reach the container.

## Upgrade first

1. **Run `./install.sh` again from this release.** A box with its own operator files names them as before (`CLIENT_KEYS_FILE=... IDENTITY_MAP_FILE=...`). One image moves by tag and digest:

   | Image | 1.6.1 | 1.6.2 |
   |---|---|---|
   | typryx (the typed plane and the risk-signal proxy) | v0.5.1 | v0.6.0 |

2. **A box on the stub, on Jev or on a local model needs nothing else.** With the three settings empty, the request stays what it always was.
3. **A box whose `TYPED_MODEL_URL` points at OpenAI moves before 2027-01-14.** OpenAI retires `gpt-4.1-mini` and `gpt-4o-mini` that day and has already shut `gpt-4.1-nano` down. Set `TYPED_MODEL_NAME` to a current model, `gpt-6-luna` for one, and add three lines to `.env`:

   ```sh
   TYPRYX_OPENAI_TOP_LOGPROBS=5
   TYPRYX_OPENAI_MAX_COMPLETION_TOKENS=16
   TYPRYX_OPENAI_REASONING_EFFORT=none
   ```

   The lines are yours and survive a change of mode, so take them out when the URL moves back to a local server. `gpt-4.1-mini` refuses them.

## What changed

- **A current OpenAI model answers through typryx with nothing in between.** It refuses `max_tokens` by name, refuses more than five alternatives, and reports token probabilities only at `reasoning_effort: none`. typryx v0.6.0 takes all three as settings, and they are named in `environment:` on `typryx` and on `typryx-wardryx-proxy`, because compose passes a container only what it names.
- **A near-certain answer is no longer thrown away.** Those models report in steps of 2^-18, so an almost certain token can come back a few steps above zero. typryx refused that as `bad_logprobs`: 13 of 60 answers on `gpt-5.6-luna`. It now counts up to 0.001 as rounding.
- **`typed-data-mode.sh` and `typed-risk-signal.sh` hold the three settings,** with a teeth case for each service.
