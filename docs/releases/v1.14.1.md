# stack-single 1.14.1

A patch release. The readers of the whole event bus get room for it: the keyryx witness, the chain verifier (agent-conform) and the notifier (heraldyx) move from a 256 MiB memory ceiling to 1 GiB. TokenFuse moves to v1.27.0.

## Upgrade first

1. **Run the installer.** No new file, no new setting. The three bus readers are recreated with their new ceiling, and every TokenFuse container (both gateway doors, the MCP broker, focus-export, the control plane) moves to v1.27.0. The new limits in TokenFuse v1.27.0 are off by default and this launcher does not turn them on. The rollback index stays 1.
2. **If 1.14.0 left the witness restarting**, this is the fix: 1.13.0 gave every service a ceiling of its own, and on a box whose bus has grown for months the witness was killed at 256 MiB (exit 137) while it re-hashed every stream, so the install's checks ended with a failure and the journal kept the earlier release as its last good one. Running the 1.14.1 installer over that box recreates the witness at 1 GiB and ends with every check passed.
3. **Going back to 1.14.0** needs nothing special, and brings the 256 MiB ceilings back.

## What changed

- **Memory ceilings for the bus readers** (invariant 85): `keyryx-witness`, `agent-conform` and `heraldyx` at 1g, beside idryx, verdryx-slo, verdryx-drift and record-seal, the other readers of the whole bus. The gate holds all of them at 1 GiB or more, and a teeth case puts the witness back at 256m and requires the gate to fail.

- **TokenFuse v1.27.0**: `tokenfuse` v1.26.0 `7080f921` to v1.27.0 `9ada89b4`, `tokenfuse-control-plane` v1.26.0 `a0f292a0` to v1.27.0 `d8ad6e9a`, by tag and digest read from ghcr.io.

## Evidence

- Found on forge on 2026-11-25 upgrading 1.12.0 to 1.14.0: `agent-stack-keyryx-witness-1` in `Restarting (137)`, seventeen restarts and more, every other container on its pin and healthy.
- CI on the release head: every install leg, `browser`, `upgrade`, `upgrade-tool-hold` and `piped`.
