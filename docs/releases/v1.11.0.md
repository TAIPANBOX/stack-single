# stack-single 1.11.0

A minor release that gives every hold an end. wardryx moves to v2.8.0: a hold nobody decides is closed as `expired` once it has waited `WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL` (24 hours, the default this release keeps), with no token, and the held agent may withdraw its own hold. An admin key cancels nothing; a person who does not want a call to go ahead denies it. The console moves to `genaryx-console:v1.14.0`, which shows those two closures as "Expired, nobody decided in time" and "Withdrawn by the agent", in a neutral colour, instead of as a refusal.

## Upgrade first

1. **Run the installer.** No new file, no new setting, no new network. Two images move by tag and digest. The rollback index stays 1.
2. **Holds older than 24 hours close at wardryx's first start.** Every pending hold that has already waited the lifetime is closed as `expired`, with no token and one `approval_expired` event each, and its place under the pending cap is free again. To keep such holds open longer, set `WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL` in `.env` (a Go duration up to `168h`) before the run, and decide them in that time.
3. **Going back to 1.10.0 needs nothing special.** The rollback index stays 1. wardryx v2.7.0 keeps the rows already closed as `expired` or `withdrawn` closed, and console v1.12.0 shows them in the refusal colour; holds go back to living until a person decides them.

Every other image is the one 1.10.0 pins. Each was read from the registry with `docker buildx imagetools inspect` and its provenance verified with `gh attestation verify`; the installer verifies each signature against its release workflow before it pulls.

## What is new

- **The hold lifetime and the withdraw route** come with wardryx v2.8.0 (its invariant 52). Nothing on this box sets `WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL`, so wardryx's own default, 24 hours, applies.
- **The console** is built from genaryx `cb7f779` (its invariant 49) with the engines this release pins.
- **The pins**: wardryx v2.8.0 and genaryx-console v1.14.0, in every place either is pinned. The gates that name a pin (`images-are-verified.sh` and the pin cases of `gates-have-teeth.sh`) read the new ones.
